🎮 Superman vs Batman – How to Play

🎯 How to Play

  • Player 1 (Superman): Use W A S D for movement, F to shoot.
  • Player 2 (Batman): Use Arrow Keys for movement, L to throw Bat Ring.
  • Goal: Reduce your opponent’s health to zero.
  • Each player has a health bar visible on the screen.
  • First to defeat the opponent wins the match.
