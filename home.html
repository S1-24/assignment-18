About Me

Hi, I'm S.J, a passionate developer who loves building user-friendly desktop applications. I enjoy working with Python, Tkinter, and Flask, and I believe in clean, modern UI design that makes technology accessible to everyone.

Project Idea

The Legendaryoneapex Bookstore project is a desktop app designed to help users manage their personal media collections, including books and films. It features a Tkinter-based frontend, a Flask backend, and a simple JSON database for easy data management. My goal was to create a reliable, intuitive system that makes organizing and searching your library effortless.
